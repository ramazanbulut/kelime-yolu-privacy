Privacy Policy

Kelime Atlası · Effective date: November 2, 2026

This policy explains what information Kelime Atlası ("the game", "we") uses and why. We keep it as small as we can.

1. No account, no personal details

You do not need an account to play, and we never ask for your name, email address or phone number.

2. Data stored on your device

Your level progress, coins, daily reward streak and your language, sound, music and vibration settings are saved only on your device. This data is not sent to us or to anyone else. Uninstalling the game or clearing its app data deletes it.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game shows ads through Google AdMob: a short ad between stops and optional ads you can watch for free coins. To show and measure these ads, Google may collect and process information such as:

  • your device's advertising ID,
  • your IP address and an approximate location derived from it,
  • device and app information (for example device model, operating system and language),
  • how you interact with ads.

Google uses this information to deliver ads, limit how often you see them, measure them and prevent fraud. If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the game asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown; if you do not consent, you may still see non-personalised ads. On iOS, the advertising ID is only shared if you allow tracking.

How Google uses data from apps that use its services: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
Google Privacy Policy: policies.google.com/privacy

4. Your choices

  • Where consent is required, you can change your ad choice at any time by clearing the game's app data or reinstalling it; the game will then ask you again.
  • On Android you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings. On iOS you can turn off "Allow Apps to Request to Track" in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.

5. No analytics or purchases

The game does not use analytics or tracking tools of its own and has no in-app purchases.

6. Internet

You can play without an internet connection. Ads are only loaded when you are online.

7. Children

The game is meant for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the game, please contact us and we will help remove it.

8. Data retention

Game data stays on your device until you delete it. Data collected by Google for advertising is kept according to Google's own policies.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal data, or to object to its use. As we do not hold personal data ourselves, most requests are handled by the settings above or by Google. You can still contact us at any time and we will help.

10. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published in the game and at this web address, with a new effective date.
